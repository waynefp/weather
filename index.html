Atmospheric Phenomena

Nature's Most
Spectacular Events

From silent auroras to devastating tornadoes, Earth's atmosphere is in constant, dramatic motion.

Electrical

Lightning
& Thunderstorms

A lightning bolt is 5× hotter than the sun's surface, reaching 30,000 K in microseconds. The rapid heating expands air at supersonic speeds, creating thunder.

⚡ 30,000 K 🌍 1.4B strikes/yr ⏱ 0.2ms duration
Geomagnetic

Aurora
Borealis

Solar winds carrying charged particles interact with Earth's magnetosphere at 60–200 km altitude, exciting atmospheric gases into a luminous curtain of dancing light.

🌌 60–200 km ☀️ Solar wind 🌡 –80°C
Severe

Tornado
& Supercell

Rotating thunderstorms can spawn tornadoes with winds exceeding 500 km/h. The mesocyclone extends from cloud base to the ground, creating nature's most violent storm.

🌪 500 km/h max ⚡ EF5 category 🗺 Tornado Alley
Tropical

Hurricane
& Cyclones

Warm ocean water fuels massive rotating storms. The calm eye — 30–65 km wide — belies the 300+ km/h eyewall winds that surround it.

🌊 26°C ocean threshold 🌀 Category 5
Low Visibility

Fog &
Advection Mist

When warm moist air flows over cooler surfaces, water vapor condenses into suspended droplets at ground level, reducing visibility to under 1 km. The stillness is deceiving.

👁 <1 km visibility 💧 RH 100%
Winter

Blizzard &
Snowstorm

When sustained winds of 56+ km/h combine with heavy snowfall and temperatures below −12°C, a blizzard emerges — whiteout conditions with wind chill factors lethal in minutes.

❄️ –12°C threshold 💨 56+ km/h winds
